Signal 1 — Brier plateau at ~0.20 (FLAT)

Brier went 0.1995 → 0.1883 → 0.1960 → 0.2000 across 150 → 300. No descending slope. The model's learning_curve.py docstring is unambiguous about this regime: "Brier flat above 0.05 → MORE DATA WON'T SAVE US, features lack IC." The activation gate of Brier < 0.05 is mathematically unreachable from this trajectory; even the relaxed "realistic regime" fallback of Brier < 0.18 is not met.

A well-specified model with real IC sees AUC stabilize or improve with more data, as test-set variance shrinks toward the true underlying ranking quality. Ours peaked at N=200 then declined. This is the mathematical signature of small-sample overfitting melting away as out-of-sample data arrives. The 0.787 at N=200 was the illusion; 0.750 at N=300 is closer to the population truth — and the trend implies it will keep drifting toward ~0.70 with more N.

Signal 3 — Feature count collapse (5 → 3 at N=300)

The null-importance permutation test (100 shuffles, 95th percentile threshold) rejected 2 of the 5 features when fed the full N=300 sample. Sprint 13c orthogonal features (flow_acceleration, event_relative_flow) and Sprint 14e absorption variants (book_absorption_*) — the features specifically engineered to capture order-book microstructure alpha — failed the null test. The regression is telling us in its native language that these measurements are statistically indistinguishable from random shuffles of the labels.

Signal 4 — Persistent U-shape monotonicity inversion

Model emitted: ✗ 2 monotonicity inversions — U-shape may persist. Do NOT lower CRITICAL threshold.

This is the same diagnostic that fired pre-Sprint-13c. The intrinsically orthogonal features were specifically designed to break the base-rate U-shape (where score vs entry price had inverted curvature). They did not. Score-vs-price is still dominated by base-rate conflation, which means the "edge" the composite score appears to detect is mostly the artifact of longshots-lose / favorites-win base rates, not directional information.

Signal 5 — OOS confidence interval includes 50%

Model emitted: WARNING: OOS win rate CI [18.6%, 49.9%] includes 50% — cannot reject null hypothesis of no edge

The strictest statistical test. At N=300, the model's out-of-sample win-rate confidence interval has 49.9% as its upper bound — meaning we cannot statistically reject the hypothesis that the model has zero predictive power. The lower bound at 18.6% is wide due to small N, but the upper bound touching 50% is the structural kill signal.

6. Kill Criteria — How the System Knew to Stop Itself

The Sprint 14g two-tier kill switch was designed for exactly this scenario:

  • Tier 1 — Cumulative hard cap ($50): NEVER auto-resets. Hitting it means strategy must be re-validated before trading resumes. Fired 2026-05-08.
  • Tier 2 — Daily soft cap ($20): Resets at UTC midnight. Designed to halt intraday blowouts.

The Tier-1 firing is not a bug or a glitch — it is the system performing its designed function: "the strategy has reached its structural risk tolerance; require human review before continuing." A trading system that cannot stop itself is the most dangerous failure mode in retail quant. DIAMOND stopped itself.

7. What We Learned — The Institutional Assets

The strategy failed. The infrastructure succeeded brilliantly. Every piece of armor below transfers directly to BERYL/CITRINE on Day 1.

7.1 Quantitative methodology

  • Point-in-Time (PiT) cross-validation — pit_mask.sum() >= 50 per fold. Prevents lookahead bias. Refuses to score folds that don't have enough strictly-prior settled trades. The "0 attempted folds" at N=50/100 is a feature, not a bug.
  • Cohort-burn discipline — burned 733 contaminated trades across Sprints 14e/14f/14h rather than mix regimes. Preserved dataset stationarity. Cost 7-10 days each time; gained statistical validity.
  • Null-importance permutation test — 100 shuffles, 95th percentile threshold. Lets the regression honestly tell you which features have no signal.
  • Two-Gate Stack epistemology — Information gate (Brier < 0.05) AND variance gate (Jaccard ≥ 0.70). Either alone is insufficient. Codified after Phase C empirical disaster (−$477 / 55% DD on bucket-WR + Kelly).
  • Pre-registered falsifiable thresholds — the N=300 Brier matrix (<0.18 = signal / 0.20-0.22 = inconclusive / >0.23 = dead) was committed BEFORE the data landed. Prevents Rorschach-test interpretation of results.

7.2 Execution infrastructure

  • Three-layer data integrity — code fix + application guard + schema CHECK constraint, non-overlapping failure domains. Simultaneous failure requires three independent bugs. 0 firings since 2026-04-16.
  • Two-tier kill switch — daily soft cap + cumulative hard cap, with explicit reset semantics. The cumulative cap saved us from a multi-hundred-dollar bleed when the strategy proved unprofitable.
  • Task-death watchdog (Sprint 14h) — asyncio.create_task with a done_callback that fires CRITICAL Pushover on any unexpected task death. Makes silent failure of async background loops mathematically impossible.
  • CancelledError-aware exception handling — explicit catching of asyncio.CancelledError (a BaseException) inside long-running poll loops, with logging + continuation when running=True. Prevents the May 1 silent task death class permanently.
  • Telemetry against absence — _book_absorption_stale canary, heartbeat logs every N iterations, watchdog file-mtime checks. The system surfaces silent failure, not just loud failure.

7.3 Operator-side lessons

  • When the comfortable explanation doesn't fit the facts, the explanation is wrong, not the facts. Pushed back on three "thin schedule" / "warmup window" / "lagging telemetry" rationalizations that turned out to mask Sprints 14e/14f/14g bugs.
  • Monitor for absence, not just presence. "Is the service running?" is a useless check. "Is the service doing real work?" is the right check.
  • Burnout is a real failure mode. 4 sandbox-era bugs in 9 days at Sprint 14e/f/g/h tempo is unsustainable. Build margin into the cadence.
  • Separate the expected value of the strategy from the expected value of the engineer. The strategy failed at retail scale. The engineer learned PiT CV, asyncio race conditions, and cohort hygiene — skills that compound on future projects.
